# Metronome Hall

Music-box architecture, clock pendulums, instrument assemblies, articulated
animations and skinned bellows are original project code. There are no human or
humanoid performers. Shared locally bundled textures retain their credited
licenses in ../../LICENSES.md. Audio credits are in ../../../audio/CREDITS.md.
Lighting generated locally.

The carved violin is a simplified CC0 scan from the Virtual Museums of Małopolska,
distributed by Oded Stein. Its pinned source and attribution are recorded in
licenses/museum-violin.md and manifest.json. The air-pump model is a Kenney CC0
asset; its source, hash and license evidence
are recorded in manifest.json. All models are bundled locally for play.

# Clockwork Citadel

Architecture, geometry, animation and material shaders are original project code.
No new third-party models or course music were added. The world uses the existing
locally bundled course textures; their ambientCG CC0 sources and conversion are
listed in ../../LICENSES.md and ../../manifest.json. Shared racer licenses remain
in ../shared/LICENSES.md. Lighting is generated locally from the authored scene.
